# MontroneDSP identity review

**H3.2 is the lead signature. K2.2 is the companion panel direction.** H3.2 suits a large manufacturer signature, a document cover or a splash screen. K2.2 has a familiar block silhouette and gives the letters more separation at small panel sizes. Both remain proposals for visual review. ## Starting point

The original header at website revision `f09fbabd46347aaf86e50dc11c354398f5bc396d` contains a 112.2 × 122.2 diamond including its perimeter. The principal diagonals rise about 47.4°. Four tonal quarters meet at the horizontal and vertical axes. The diamond-shaped hole measures 44.08 × 47.88 in the source coordinates. The outer shape is centered geometrically. Its light upper half makes the visual center rise on the dark header. At 16 px the lower-right facet nearly disappears into the background. A perimeter stroke carries much of that edge. [Original vector](00-original/original-header-logo.svg) · [Isolated emblem](00-original/original-icon.svg)

The solid silhouette, narrow O counters and lowered DSP are the strongest points of continuity. The original icon was about 1.92 times the main flat cap height, with a painted gap of approximately 30 units before M. At normal header size it reads as a separate badge. The first-stage archive is in commit `d5ea4a21034f1ae1a4d778b8a2644ea0ae029a97`. Four Phase 1 whitespace-only lines were deliberately retained in the byte-identical source copy. ## How the geometry evolved

| Family | What the drawing tested | Judgment |
|---|---|---|
| A / A2 | One solid, clipped diamond. Icon height went from 148 to 141 per 100 units of cap height. Gap went from 36 to 31. | Closest to the old outline. A2 clears the center, but the ring has little individual character. |
| B / B2 / B3 | Broader outer flats and seats cut into the aperture. Height went from 131 to 124 to 123. | The one-piece shape prints well. The upright axis still feels separate from the italic name. |
| C / C2 | Two open bridges. Height went from 116 to 119. Gap went from 22 to 25. | Readable at icon size. The break suggests a directional chevron more readily than the other constructions. |
| J1–J3 | A leaning lozenge, a rounded shoulder and an open fold. | J1 establishes the inclination. J2 approaches an O-shaped ring. J3 reads as a G and was set aside. |
| K1–K2F | Close spacing, an ascending connection to M, and a lower release. | The ascending connection preserves the diamond. The low release in K3 suggests a Q. K2F is the drawing used in the earlier application proofs. |
| H1 / H2 / H2F | Capital signatures using both diamonds as O and joining every pair. | The changing heights of the connections add movement. A long row of capitals is more formal than the mixed-case drawing. |
| H3 / H3F / H3.2 | A tall M, low diamond o’s, lowercase bowls and a rising t. | The mixed-case rhythm carries the signature. The final t–r and e–D transitions are drawn into the letters themselves. |
| K2.2 | A second pass on the K2F panel drawing. | The diamond opening is wider on the side carrying the join. The adjoining M is drawn closer, which keeps the neck short. |

## H3.2 · Mixed-case signature

The repeated diamond supplies the two o’s at the actual reading positions in **MontroneDSP**. Each measures about 56.94 × 78.31 on a 100-unit capital grid. The points sit just outside the lowercase body, with their lower tips close to the common baseline. The 8.53° incline belongs to the lettering as a whole. Each diamond remains identifiable when lifted out for the avatar.

The first M has a lightly flared foot. The stem of t rises above the other lowercase letters and sweeps directly into r. The r carries the longest low stroke into the second diamond. The e releases into D with one rising curve. Both diamond–n pairs land on the same lower-right facet, avoiding the small hooks visible in the first mixed-case drawing. DSP has nominal 65-unit capitals and remains connected from e through P. There is no prefix gap in this composition. The icon is already a letter.

The editable master keeps the named letters, diamond placements and joining strokes. The display and cutting geometry has one connected ink body with five open counters. At full width the repeated apertures give the name its rhythm. At the 100 px website width the fine joins and lowercase r are near their smallest useful size. ## K2.2 · Panel lockup

K2.2 develops the K2F application proof identified in the review. The leading diamond is 92.22 × 107.40 on the same capital grid and spans y = −3.8 through 103.6. Its center sits at 49.9 beside the caps’ center at 50. The main M body starts at x = 98.4, 1.6 units closer than K2F. The roughly six-unit span from the emblem’s right bound to the M’s nominal origin is bridged by a rising stroke. It reads as contact, with a pocket of negative space above and below.

The opening in the diamond gained 4.5% in width and 2.5% in height. It moves slightly toward the joined shoulder to balance the ink at that corner. The M’s entry meets the stem at a tangent. The remaining capitals retain their 5.2° slope. DSP uses 70.4-unit capitals, a 4.9-unit baseline drop and slightly more space after E. The grouped source separates the diamond from the lettering. The display master welds their intentional contact for one-ink output. [Editable lockup](06-delivery/K2.2/editable.svg) · [Display / cutting master](06-delivery/K2.2/lockup-black.svg)

## Selection

Scores are optical judgments on a ten-point scale. The direction of the latest review gives letter recognition and the requested humanist movement particular importance within the integration and craftsmanship scores.

| Drawing | Professional identity 25% | Integration 20% | Lettering 15% | Continuity 15% | Balance 15% | Reproduction 10% | Weighted |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A2 | 7 | 6 | 7 | 9 | 7 | 8 | 7.20 |
| B3 | 8 | 6 | 8 | 7 | 7 | 8 | 7.30 |
| C2 | 6 | 7 | 7 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 6.70 |
| H3, first drawing | 8 | 8 | 7 | 7 | 7 | 7 | 7.45 |
| H3.2 | 9 | 9 | 9 | 7 | 9 | 7 | 8.50 |
| K2.2 | 9 | 8 | 8 | 9 | 9 | 9 | 8.65 |

The numerical advantage of K2.2 comes from continuity and reproduction. H3.2 better expresses the requested humanist character. The two drawings merit separate review in their intended settings. The open fold and the low diamond tail were excluded on recognition grounds. The shortlisted marks get their movement from the stroke direction and return curves.

## Application and reproduction checks

The shared website header was rendered locally in Windows Edge with only the header image request substituted. The desktop image slot is 170 CSS px wide and the mobile slot is 100. Both finalists were tested on the home page, SWARA XT, MX2 and the narrow Contact header at widths of 1440, 390, 1280, 1024 and 320 CSS px, including device scale factors of 1, 1.25, 1.5 and 2. Separate light-paper and dark-ink studies show polarity. The instrument panel proof uses the website’s existing SWARA XT Windows screenshot. Only the manufacturer artwork region was composited for the review.
