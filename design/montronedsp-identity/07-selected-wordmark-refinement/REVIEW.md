# Selected wordmark — professional refinements

**R02 is the proposed master.** The narrower n repeats the diamond’s light–dark rhythm. Its shoulder reaches full weight later, so the eye can follow the opening into the next letter. The tall M and ascending t still establish the two accents in the name.

| Drawing | Shoulder and r | Ending |
|---|---|---|
| R01 | A smaller n arch with a familiar rounded r terminal. Both n stems keep the original baseline. | Original D and P. The S exits directly into P. |
| R02 | Shorter n advance, less weight on the return and a lifted underside to the r shoulder. Slightly larger diamond counters and lighter right joins. | The e sweeps into D. D and S carry their joining curves in their own contours. |
| R03 | The narrowest n and a small, rounded turn at the top of r. The diamond tips extend slightly further. | The e finishes with an open upstroke. A short interval lets the three capitals read separately. |

The first pass exposed small protrusions where the former D–S and S–P bridges overlapped the bowls. The final drawings absorb the S exit into the letter. R02 and R03 also absorb the D exit. The first pass and all its component outlines are retained.

The inclination is 9.65 degrees. At the design scale of 100 for the M, the R02 first n has a 13.1-unit entry stem at y = 65. Widths and overshoots are marked on the construction sheet. Both n letters have the same body drawing and advance corrections appropriate to their following t or e.

In the 100 px header the differences between r terminals are subtle. The larger white-on-dark proofs show their shape more clearly. Use the standalone signature for the header. For a combined identity, the 60-unit icon with 22 units of clearance leaves the M prominent. At 83 the third diamond competes with the opening stroke. The compact 46-unit icon has a smaller aperture at reduction. The single-ink symbol uses the four filled planes of the archived corporate diamond.

Each drawing includes grouped editable outlines, solid display SVGs, transparent 2600 px PNGs, a header proof, and a separate icon-and-wordmark lockup. The website header sheet uses local Edge captures at 1440 and 390 CSS pixels, with the image response set to each candidate. The live local preview shows the selected original.
