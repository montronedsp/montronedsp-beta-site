# H3.3 · joined DSP lettering

The S–P edge repair is the parent of this drawing. The D releases into S high on the bowl. Its two edges follow the curves of both capitals, giving the opening beneath the connection a longer sweep. The lower S–P stroke travels a little farther down before turning up along the stem of P. A small curved turn on the underside keeps the cut open.

The D and P have slightly more space inside their bowls. The two exposed ends of S take shorter, gently curved cuts. The letter positions, the H3.3 lettering through the e and its contact with D are preserved.
