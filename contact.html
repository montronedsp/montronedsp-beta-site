Help us understand your setup.

A few details in your first email help us give you a useful answer.

  1. Your instrument and system

    Tell us which product you are using, its version, and your operating system: macOS, Windows or Linux. Include the DAW or host name and version, or say if you are using a standalone build.

  2. What you are trying to do

    Describe the question or problem, what you expected, and what happened. If you can reproduce it, include the steps.

  3. Useful details

    Attach a screenshot or the exact error message when it helps. For installation questions, tell us which package and plugin format you installed.

Orders and licensing

For an order or activation question, include the email address used at checkout and the product name. An order reference, if you have one, helps us find the purchase.
