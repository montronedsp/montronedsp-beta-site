Store security

You evaluate instruments on MontroneDSP. When you choose to purchase, checkout is completed through Gumroad - a dedicated commerce platform - so payment is handled by specialised infrastructure rather than by this website.

Last updated: 25 September 2026

MontroneDSP is an independent studio. We do not operate our own payment gateway. The store is powered by Gumroad so that cards, receipts, and delivery sit with a provider built for that work.

How a purchase works

Product pages, sound examples, manuals, and technical detail live on montronedsp.com. That is the studio site: the place to understand an instrument before you buy it.

When you select Buy, checkout is opened through Gumroad. Gumroad is the commerce layer: it takes the order, processes payment, issues the receipt, and delivers the files or license associated with that purchase.

This separation is deliberate. We keep the catalogue, writing, and product experience here. Payment stays with a platform whose purpose is secure retail.

Why Gumroad

Gumroad is an established digital-commerce provider used by independent software makers and publishers. It is not a decorative storefront we ask you to browse first; it is the checkout that sits behind the Buy action on each product page.

Using Gumroad means card data is processed in an environment designed for that task, with encrypted connections and payment partners who operate under industry card-handling standards. MontroneDSP does not run its own card terminal, and we do not receive or store your payment card numbers.

What you can expect

  • Encrypted checkout over HTTPS
  • Payment processed by Gumroad and its banking and card partners - not entered into a form on this website
  • A purchase receipt from the store, with access to your files and license from your Gumroad library
  • A seven-day money-back guarantee on store purchases

No checkout is risk-free in the abstract. What we can state clearly is that we do not ask you to send card details by email, and we do not keep a copy of your card on our servers.

What MontroneDSP receives

After an order, we may receive the information needed to fulfil it and to support you afterwards: typically the product bought, the date, the email used at checkout, and any license or download details attached to that sale.

That record exists so we can help with installation, activation, and refunds. It is not a second copy of your card.

Privacy and further reading

How Gumroad handles purchase and payment data is described in Gumroad’s Privacy Policy. How MontroneDSP treats personal data more broadly - including licenses and support - is in our Privacy Policy.

If something about an order looks wrong, write to support@montronedsp.com. We would rather hear from you than leave a doubt unattended.
